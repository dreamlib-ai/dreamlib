# Code of Conduct

Dreamlib is a place where people share something personal: how they dream and what their cultures say about it. We want it to stay kind, curious and safe.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Give and take feedback gracefully.
- Treat every dream tradition, faith and culture with respect. We describe them; we do not rank or ridicule them.
- Treat dreams as private. Do not share someone else's dream, and do not speculate about a person from theirs.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including their dreams, without permission.
- Mocking a person's beliefs or the way their culture interprets dreams.
- Using the project to diagnose, pathologize or shame anyone.

## Where this applies

In the repository, in issues and pull requests, in community chats, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If something goes wrong, tell the maintainers privately through the contact listed on the repository's community page. Reports are kept confidential. We will listen, ask what you need, and act.

## What happens next

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may warn or ban people temporarily or permanently. Decisions are made to protect the community, starting with the least severe step that fixes the problem.
